# Chapter 5: Unlocking Superpowers - The Capabilities Guide

Welcome, hero, to the capabilities guide. In this chapter, you'll learn how to unlock your provider's hidden potential with capabilities. Capabilities are like superpowers that you can add to your provider to give it new abilities.

## 💥 What are Capabilities?

Capabilities are reusable components that can be used to extend the functionality of your provider. They can be used to add new features to your provider, such as:

-   **Authentication:** Add support for new authentication methods, such as OAuth or API keys.
-   **Logging:** Add custom logging to your provider.
-   **Caching:** Add caching to your provider to improve performance.
-   **And much more!**

## 🚀 How do they work?

Capabilities are discovered and registered with the `hub` just like resources, data sources, and functions. This means that you can easily create and share capabilities between your providers.

When you add a capability to your provider, `pyvider` will automatically call the `setup` method of the capability. This is where you can add new resources, data sources, and functions to your provider.
