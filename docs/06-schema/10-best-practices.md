# ✨ Best Practices

This guide provides best practices for designing clean and maintainable schemas.

## 📄 Keep it Simple

The best schemas are simple and easy to understand. Avoid creating complex schemas with many nested blocks and attributes.

## 📄 Be Consistent

Use a consistent naming convention for your attributes and blocks. This will make your schema easier to read and understand.

## 📄 Be Descriptive

Use the `description` attribute to provide a clear and descriptive explanation of your attributes and blocks. This will help your users understand how to use your provider.

## 📄 Use Validators

Use validators to enforce constraints on the values in your schema. This will help to prevent errors and ensure that your provider is used correctly.
